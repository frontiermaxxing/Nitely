# Retro: 2026-10-16

What one day of agents building Nitely through `submit` and `land` cost, from Nitely's own
database, read only: events, reviews, lands, and `nitely spend`. The day covers 20 h, 116
reviewed issues, 112 lands (about 190 commits on main) and 26.8M tokens (claude 22.4M, codex
4.4M), so about 207k tokens per land.

## Where the time and tokens went

**1. Review rounds.** 96 of 305 reviews asked for changes. 58 of 116 issues needed at least one
changes round. Tokens per issue (median) rise with the rounds:

```
rounds  issues  median tokens
1       33       63k  ███
2       39       94k  ████▌
3-4     30      188k  █████████
5+      14      355k  █████████████████
```

The 14 issues with 5 or more rounds used 7.9M of the 23.5M tokens tied to an issue (34%).
Round 1 raised 79 P0/P1 findings. Rounds 2 and later raised 43 more, and 39 came back carried
(still open after a fix). Waiting on review took 8.9 h in all (median 99 s each).
→ [NIT-161](nitely://issue/NIT-161)

**2. Rebase conflicts at land.** Of the 44 land stops:

```
rebase conflict              29  ██████████████
gate failed after rebase     12  ██████
branch changed since review   2  █
waits for human approval      1
```

A resolved conflict took a median of 10 min from the stop to the land, and 21 of them took 5.4 h
in all. The files in those conflicts were db.rs 9, fixtures.ts 4, App.tsx 4, visualization.md 4
and Kit.tsx 3. db.rs conflicts because every schema change edits the same three places
(`SCHEMA_VERSION`, the end of `migrate`, the end of `SCHEMA`). → [NIT-162](nitely://issue/NIT-162)

**3. Flaky gates.** 22 of the 35 gate failures later passed at the same commit with no change:
`cargo test --workspace` 17, `npm ci` after a dependency change 4, and `npm test` 1. Each one
cost a submit round or a land stop, about 6 min to recover at land.
→ [NIT-160](nitely://issue/NIT-160), built in this change

**4. Reinstalling nitely.** Agents ran `nitely-cli -- install` 131 times from 11 sessions, about
2.5 h. Land refuses to run when the installed nitely is older than main, so every worker
rebuilds it. → [NIT-163](nitely://issue/NIT-163)

## Smaller than expected

- **Land lock:** 33 explicit waits across 11 sessions, about 19 min in all. Holding the lock
  (rebase, gate and push) took a median of 115 s per land, 2.7 h over the day.
- **Bounces:** one issue went Todo → In Progress → Todo (NIT-18). Nine went from In Review back
  to In Progress after a review.
- **Gate repeats:** 418 gate passes for 112 lands. 31 of them reran a commit that had already
  passed.
- **Cycle time:** from In Progress to Done, a median of 22 min (p90 75 min).

## How it was counted

- `event`: `review_started` to `submit` gives the review time; the `Rebasing … onto main` status
  to `landed` gives the land time; `land_failed` summaries give the causes and the conflicting
  files; `gate_failed` and `gate_passed` at the same sha mark a flake; `issue_moved` gives the
  bounces; `tool` events in Nitely folders give the lock waits and installs.
- `review`: one row per submit, with verdict and findings (`carried` marks a finding raised
  again).
- Spend: `nitely spend NIT-n` for every reviewed issue, and `nitely spend --project NIT`.
