Reports arrive once a week. To be on it, open Leaderboard in the Nitely app and choose Join: you pick a display name and which projects count, and you see the exact report before anything is sent.

Get Nitely

What is sent

A weekly report of counts and rates: changes landed, review rounds, first-pass rate, lead time, agent hours, tokens, Inbox reply time, and which vendors did the work. The app shows it to you, field by field, before you send it.

What never leaves your machine

Code, file paths, issue text, repository, project and branch names, prompts, and your identity. The service keeps no addresses and no access logs.

Leaving

Stop sharing any time, or leave and delete everything you sent with one signed request from the app. There are no accounts: your install's key is the only id.
